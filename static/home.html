30-day readmission risk

A gradient boosting model that estimates the probability a diabetic inpatient is readmitted within 30 days of discharge — trained on 99,343 de-identified encounters from 130 US hospitals, served with its own limitations attached to every prediction.

Not for clinical use. This is a personal portfolio project. The data are from 1999–2008; the model has not been externally validated, and it performs near chance for patients aged 90+. Every prediction returns the limitations that apply to it.
